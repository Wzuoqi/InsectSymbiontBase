About Our Datasets

These datasets represent comprehensive collections of insect symbiont data, including genomes, metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs), and metagenomic sequences. By downloading and using these resources, you agree to cite our database in any resulting publications.

ISDB Gene from Genome

Last updated: November 2024

Complete collection of gene sequences extracted from symbiont genomes. This dataset includes annotated genes with functional classifications.

ISDB Gene from MAG

Last updated: November 2024

Gene sequences extracted from Metagenome-Assembled Genomes (MAGs) of insect symbionts, providing insights into uncultivated symbiont genetics.

3.6 GB

ISDB Gene from Meta

Last updated: November 2024

Gene sequences from metagenomes of insect microbiomes, providing a comprehensive view of the genetic diversity in insect-associated microbial communities.
